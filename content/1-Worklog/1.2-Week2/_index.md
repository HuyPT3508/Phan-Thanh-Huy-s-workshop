### Week 2 Objectives

* Organize team meetings to discuss potential project topics.
* Analyze and evaluate available open-source projects (legacy codebases) to serve as a development foundation.
* Create a shortlist of potential projects and compare their pros and cons.

### Completed Tasks

1. **Team Brainstorming:**
   * Proposed various ideas: E-commerce Web, Library Management System, Cinema Ticket Booking System, etc.
   * Analyzed the feasibility of each idea based on the team's available time and skill sets.
2. **Evaluating Legacy Codebases:**
   * Searched GitHub for repositories matching the proposed ideas.
   * Conducted test deployments of several source codes on the Local environment.
   * General consensus: Most legacy source codes had a Monolithic architecture (difficult to scale), outdated UI designs, and lacked real-time features.
3. **Deciding the Direction:**
   * Instead of starting from scratch, the team decided to select a promising legacy codebase and fully **Refactor** it towards a Cloud-Native architecture.

### Outcomes

* Narrowed the ideas down to the top 2 most feasible projects.
* Agreed on a unified technical direction for the entire team.
