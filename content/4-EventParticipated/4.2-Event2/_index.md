# Summary Report: "FCAJ - Agentic AI Build Week (AABW) Hackathon"

### Event Objectives

![FCAJ Agentic AI Build Week](/images/4-EventParticipated/4.2-Event2/45e3dad5485ec900904f3.jpg)

* Listen to competing teams present innovative solutions applying Agentic AI to solve real-world problems.
* Learn about building cloud architectures on AWS, integrating multi-channel AI, and intelligent data processing.

### Highlighted Projects

#### 1. KFC Bot Agent - AI-Powered Conversation Ordering (One Team)
![Ordering Without Leaving the Chat](/images/4-EventParticipated/4.2-Event2/1c37c305518ed0d0899f7.jpg)
* **Solution**: Built a multi-channel AI Agent that supports automated ordering directly on Zalo, Messenger, and WhatsApp without requiring users to switch apps (Ordering Without Leaving the Chat).
* **Takeaway**: Optimized User Experience (UX) through natural conversation, reducing friction and reliance on human support agents.

#### 2. Adaptive AML Workflow Engine (Anti-Money Laundering)
![Adaptive AML Workflow Engine](/images/4-EventParticipated/4.2-Event2/1aa1de974c1ccd42940d2.jpg)
![Traditional AML Workflow](/images/4-EventParticipated/4.2-Event2/bdaf399fab142a4a73055.jpg)
* **Context**: Traditional Anti-Money Laundering (AML) workflows are highly time-consuming (~3 hours/case), require manual report reading, and are limited by human capacity.
* **Solution**: Applied AI to automate the analysis process, increasing Speed, Explainability (with data backed by source citations), and Scalability (handling exponentially more cases without a proportional increase in headcount).

#### 3. Hackathon Journey - 24 Hours of Building, Failing, and Learning (Team 3KA)
![What Our Team Will Share](/images/4-EventParticipated/4.2-Event2/38c13ff1ad7a2c24756b4.jpg)
![Hackathon Journey](/images/4-EventParticipated/4.2-Event2/0a4eee7b7cf0fdaea4e11.jpg)
* A presentation sharing the grueling 24-hour coding journey, the challenges overcome, and the realization that the best lessons often come from failures and exhaustion rather than just winning.

#### 4. Other Innovative Solutions
* **Signal Scout**: Solutions related to signal processing and data analysis using AI.
* **Native App (Team Plan V)**: Proposed solutions involving Native Applications combined with Agentic AI.

### System Architecture

![Architecture Diagram](/images/4-EventParticipated/4.2-Event2/77f9c3cb5140d01e89518.jpg)
![Architecture Diagram 2](/images/4-EventParticipated/4.2-Event2/c378db4949c2c89c91d36.jpg)
* During the event, teams presented complex Architecture Diagrams heavily utilizing the AWS ecosystem (API Gateway, Lambda, DynamoDB, Bedrock, etc.).
* Designing Microservices combined with Serverless architectures ensures the system operates flexibly and scales rapidly.

### Experiences and Key Takeaways
* **Teamwork and Time Pressure**: Through the presentations, I learned how participating teams demonstrated immense effort and problem-solving skills under the intense pressure of a short-term hackathon.
* **Applying AI to Business Problems**: Instead of solely focusing on core technology, teams actively tried to apply AI to directly resolve business pain points (e.g., fast ordering, anti-money laundering).
* **Cloud-native Architecture Design**: Gained a deeper understanding of combining AWS services to create complete, secure, and cost-optimized solutions.
